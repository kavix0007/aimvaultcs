Best CS2 Crosshair Colors and RGB Settings

Learn how CS2 crosshair colors work and how to choose a color that stays visible across maps.

CS2 color options

CS2 supports preset crosshair colors and custom RGB values. Custom RGB is useful when the standard presets do not give enough contrast for your monitor and maps.

Use contrast, not fashion

Green and cyan are common choices, but the best color for you is the one that remains visible without distracting you. Check it against both light and dark surfaces.

Keep alpha in mind

Alpha controls opacity. Fully opaque crosshairs are easier to see, while lower alpha can reduce visual weight. Test the result at your normal brightness and resolution.
