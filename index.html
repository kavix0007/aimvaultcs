What is a CS2 crosshair?

Your crosshair is the fixed reference point you aim with in Counter-Strike 2. Unlike a mouse cursor, it stays centered on your screen while the world moves around it, so its color, size, and shape directly affect how quickly you can confirm a shot is lined up.

How to choose a crosshair

Start with visibility: a crosshair only helps if you can see it instantly against every map background. From there, tune size and gap for your role — smaller and tighter for long-range precision, slightly larger for close-range tracking — using the generator to preview changes live.

Professional CS2 crosshairs

Pro players tend to favor compact, high-contrast crosshairs with a dark outline for consistency across lighting conditions. Browsing the Pro Settings database is a good way to see common patterns by role, not a guarantee any single setup will suit your own sensitivity or monitor.

Crosshair settings explained

Color, size, gap, thickness, outline, and center dot each change how your crosshair reads on screen. Read our guide on how crosshair gap works for a deeper breakdown of the setting players misunderstand most.

Crosshair and settings data on AIMVAULT is community-tracked for demonstration purposes and is not verified against any player's current or official configuration. Team names, roles, and settings may be outdated — always treat entries as a starting point, not a confirmed live setup.
